About

PayFlow is an educational model of a credit card processing system. It follows the use-case flow of a cardholder, merchant and system: enter and validate card details, enter an amount, authorize, process, verify the transaction and generate a receipt. It demonstrates form validation, transaction management, status tracking and live dashboard updates.
